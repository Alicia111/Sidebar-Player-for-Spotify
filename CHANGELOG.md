# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Now Playing sidebar view with cover art, seekable progress bar, playback controls, shuffle, repeat, volume, like button and device switcher
- Search songs, albums, artists and playlists, then play, queue, like or add results to a playlist
- Artist pages with albums and singles, opened from artist names
- Queue view showing the playback source and upcoming tracks; click one to jump to it
- Playlists view with Recently Played, Liked Songs and your playlists, plus a context menu to queue, like, add to or remove from a playlist
- Devices view and picker to move playback, including starting the Spotify desktop app on this computer
- Status bar controls and keyboard shortcuts
- Time-synced lyrics from LRCLIB in a panel beside the editor
- Listen on Spotify links on tracks, albums, artists and search results
